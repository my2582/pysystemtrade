4 · Niche-strategy reading

  • The mini/micro NAME filter was a proxy that under-counted niche. Sector indices (EU-BANKS/AUTO/CHEM …) and full-size ags/softs (COCOA/COFFEE/CORN/COTTON …) are the natural small-AUM niches but carry no "mini" in their name. Switching to DV captures them.
  • $50k clears ≥4-contract granularity on 121 of 189 instruments (15 from original mini/micro + 106 newly added), of which 15 are on current data. The capital headroom is large; the binding scarcity is current data, not money.
  • High-DV outliers (GOLD_micro in the original 22, plus a handful of full-size indices/bonds in the added set) push min-account up only if you keep them in scope. A min_contracts rule auto-excludes them when capital is insufficient.
  • Next leverage: refresh the IB backfill universe to bring the niche softs / sector / bond legs from STALE → current.
