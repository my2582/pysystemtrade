Minimum Account-Size Study — mini/micro futures (ex-FX, ex-energy)

30% vol target · 22 instruments (5 current, 17 stale) · USD-converted · generated 2026-05-27 19:59

Why this matters: account size → how many contracts you can hold at the 30% vol target → which instruments are tradeable → universe size → what strategy is viable. Binding variable is dollar-vol per contract DV = (multiplier × price) × %vol — not %vol alone. With small capital, only low-DV (small-notional) contracts clear the granularity bar — which steers naturally toward niche / less-crowded markets (ags, micro metals, sector), consistent with the small-AUM edge.
Method. MinAccount_USD(k) = k × DV_local ÷ 0.30 × fx→USD, DV_local = pointsize × dailyDiffVol × √256, where dailyDiffVol = pysystemtrade mixed_vol_calc (35d + 20y slow-vol blend) on price diffs (additive back-adjustment makes pct_change invalid — it explodes where back-adjusted prices cross zero). FX from repo fx_prices_csv. "k-contract basis" = smallest account where the 30%-vol position is ≥ k integer contracts; below k=1 the forced 1-contract position overshoots 30%.

3 · Reading it for a small-AUM niche strategy

The bottleneck is data currency, not capital. At $50k the potential universe is rich — 15 of 22 instruments clear the ≥4-contract comfort line and 21 clear ≥2 contracts. But only 2 of those are on current data at ≥4 ctr, because the 5 maintained instruments happen to be the high-DV ones (big indices + high-vol metals). Every cheap niche contract (ags, micro metals, bonds) is STALE. So $50k is plenty — the gate to a real niche rotation is refreshing the data (expand IB backfill), not raising the account.
  • The 1-ctr column is the absolute floor (below it the forced position overshoots 30% vol). The 4-ctr column is the comfort line (integer rounding within ~±12% of target).
  • Small accounts steer toward low-DV, small-notional contracts — agriculturals, micro metals, bonds, sector/vol — the less-crowded "niche" corners, exactly where the small-AUM edge lives (fast signals, micro contracts, thinner markets).
  • High-DV instruments (large index / metals in high-vol regimes) are the binding constraint — they alone push the required account up; the lone current GOLD_micro needs $160k for ≥4 ctr at today's elevated vol.
  • Single-position rotation needs cross-sectional dispersion: over a too-thin / too-correlated set it degrades to fixed-single. Today's 5 current names are 3 correlated equity indices + 2 metals → thin. Refreshing the cheap ags/bonds/metals legs both widens the universe and lowers correlation.
