F1 · ProblemSingle-position trend SR is structurally weakCarver / Martin / Hanauer-Windmüller
Sharpe ratio scales with diversification, not signal cleverness
Net Sharpe by configuration. The 0.7-1.0 paper claim contradicts Carver's empirics; we anchor on ≥0.40.

Decode the jargon

  • SR Net annualized Sharpe ratio after costs.
  • EWMAC EMA fast minus EMA slow (a.k.a. MACD), normalized by realized vol.
  • DV Dollar-volatility per contract = multiplier × price × %vol.
  • TSMOM Time-series momentum (Moskowitz-Ooi-Pedersen). NOT in the two PDFs sourced.
  • Dual mom Antonacci's relative-rank + absolute (vs T-bill) filter. NOT in the PDFs (general knowledge).

The evidence (four ways)

  1. Literature. Carver: per-instrument trend SR ≈ 0.24 pre-cost; portfolio ≈ 1.0 comes almost entirely from combining 30-100 low-correlation instruments, not from signal quality.
  2. Literature (sourced). Hanauer & Windmüller (2022), Table 2 p.35: cross-sectional equity MOM baseline US Sharpe 0.47; risk-managed variants reach 0.83-0.91. Improvements come from vol-scaling (cMOM Sharpe 0.47→0.87, MaxDD -69%→-39%) and dynamic state-conditioning (dMOM Sharpe 0.91), not from cleverer ranking.
  3. Literature (sourced). Martin (2023) p.22-23: a hard sign-flip signal is explicitly discouraged ("buys-and-dumps in non-trending markets"); a dead-zone buffer ε≈0.6 (vol-normalized units) recovers positive skew without Sharpe loss.
  4. Empirical. Pasted research summary (Perplexity sonar-deep-research) claimed 0.7-1.0; the report returned 0 citations (a known sonar-deep-research failure mode) and contradicts Carver. Flagged unverified, dismissed as the target bar.
Why it matters. Setting the success bar at 0.7-1.0 builds a strategy that cannot succeed; setting it at 0.40 is honest and lets cMOM-style vol-targeting (which we already do) and rotation diversification do the heavy lifting.

Four-stage rotation. The src/ layer applies stages 2-4 on top of pysystemtrade outputs.

The approach (justified)

  1. Universe = DV-based, not name-based: all futures with adjusted-price data, ex-FX, ex-energy (OilGas). 189 instruments captured (vs 22 in v1) - critical for small-AUM niche dispersion. scripts/universe_min_account_full.py
  2. Architecture: always-invested top-1 (owner-corrected from "cash filter"); no go-flat default. Vol-target sets integer contracts; residual capital sits in cash (normal margined-futures behaviour).
  3. Signal v1 = A/B. A: pysystemtrade-native EWMAC combined forecast as the rank score (max reuse). B: academic blended momentum 3/6/12M (signal-level averaging per Martin p.7 precedent; multi-lookback blend itself is general knowledge - PDFs do not specify a 3/6/12 recipe).
  4. Integration: pysystemtrade System cannot express top-1 + cash. A new src/arki_strategies/absmom_rotation.py consumes the engine's per-instrument forecast / vol / price / cost and emits the standard run artifacts (stats.yaml, daily_returns.csv, dashboard_meta.json) into results/runs/<id>/. Core engine untouched. get_run_summary + generate_dashboard reuse for free.

Formulas

Dollar-vol per contract (in local ccy) DVlocal = m · σd · 256    where σd = std(pt−pt−1) from engine mixed_vol_calc
Minimum account for k contracts (USD) Amin(k) = k · DVlocalvtgt · fx→USD    with vtgt = 0.30
Position in contracts at capital C N = C · vtgtm · p · σp    rounded to nearest integer
EWMAC forecast (engine, capped ±20) φt = clip±20(EMAfast(p) − EMAslow(p)σp,t)

F4 · TradeoffFat tail, thin pool, regime risk - the honest cost of single-positionno free lunch
Fat-tail drawdown 30-50% even with vol target; no diversification cushion
Expected MaxDD bands by configuration. Single-position can't get below ~30% even with overlays - this is the irreducible cost of forgoing diversification.

The honest cost (single-position)

  • fat tail 30-50% MaxDD is structural; vol-target shrinks but does not eliminate it. macro-mini's MaxDD of ~20% comes from cross-instrument cancellation that single-position cannot have.
  • least-bad Always-invested top-1 can be long the strongest-momentum instrument in a broad downturn (i.e. still falling). The dead-zone or dMOM overlay (F2) is the studied mitigation.
  • regime concentration The held instrument is the entire portfolio for that week; a structural break in that name (e.g. JGB carry trade unwind) is undiversifiable.
  • granularity At $50k, GOLD_micro vol-target wants 1.3 contracts (rounds to 1 or 2) - 30% ± ~12% rounding error. A min_contracts parameter auto-excludes instruments where N < threshold.
  • thin pool risk A 5-name pool with 3 correlated equity indices has weak cross-sectional dispersion. The DV view (F3) makes this strictly better (42 current incl. softs, bonds, sectors).

Mitigations (in spec)

  1. Dead-zone ε≈0.6 on the top-1 score (research item, F2 dead-zone formula).
  2. dMOM-style bear-state down-weight (Hanauer p.11-12, eq.6-7).
  3. min_contracts floor - excludes weeks where vol-target can't be met.
  4. DV-based universe widens the pool from 5 (correlated equities) to 15+ current at $50k ≥4-ctr.
Why we still build it. The owner's constraint is small AUM → single-position is the design space. The goal is not "beat macro-mini" - it is "beat fixed-single (SR≈0.24) by a factor we can validate, and cut tail relative to buy&hold." Both are realistic.

cMOM vol-targeting is the single biggest sourced Sharpe enhancement
Hanauer & Windmüller (2022) Table 2 p.35, US sample. cMOM / sMOM / dMOM are vol-targeting variants - not better signals.

Statistical anchors (with verdicts)

  1. Single fixed SR ≈ 0.24 (Carver). Pre-cost, point estimate. Direction-only meaningful; not significant @95% on a single-instrument sample. Sets the floor.
  2. cMOM uplift Baseline 0.47 → cMOM 0.87 (Hanauer Table 2, p.35). Skew -2.18 → -0.32. Robust - same uplift in EUR/JPN sub-samples (Table 3). Significance: clear by inspection given the magnitude; the paper's CI is wide but uplift dominates.
  3. Dead-zone ε≈0.6 recovered positive skew at no Sharpe loss (Martin p.22-23, p.27). Point + qualitative verdict only; no CI reported in the paper.
  4. Our target Net SR ≥ 0.40. Confidence: a rotation pool of 5-15 with low correlation should clear this given 30% vol-target and the cMOM-style scaling we already apply. Not a forecast - a falsifiable bar.

Overfitting quantified

  • tuned params v1 baseline (A0/B0): EWMAC speeds (default {8/32, 16/64, 32/128} - standard) + min_contracts (=1). Effectively 0 tuned. Optional: dead-zone ε (1 param), dMOM bear threshold (1 param). Total ≤ 3.
  • sample Per-instrument history 9-54 years (full history honored); cross-sectional pool 5-15. Single-position rotation has a smaller effective sample than a portfolio strategy.
  • OOS Pre-2015 vs post-2015 split required; cost-sensitivity 1x/2x spread; regime breakdown (GFC, COVID, 2022).
  • Verdict Low overfit risk at the A0/B0 baseline. Risk rises if we tune ε or dMOM threshold without OOS validation.
