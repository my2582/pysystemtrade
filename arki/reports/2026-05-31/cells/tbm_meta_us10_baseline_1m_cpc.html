Mechanism cheatsheet

1. Core formula tested

Triple-Barrier label + meta-sizer on Martin vol-normalised returns

\[U_n = \frac{X_{n+1}-X_n}{\hat{\sigma}_n}, \quad \hat{\sigma}_n^2 = \mathrm{EMA}_{\alpha=0.05}\big((X_n-X_{n-1})^2\big), \quad \mathrm{PT}=8\hat{\sigma},\; \mathrm{SL}=4\hat{\sigma},\; T_{\max}=120\,\text{bd} \]

X = ZN panama-adjusted price; σ̂ = Martin §2.5 20-day EMA of squared absolute price changes (γ=0.95 ⇒ α=0.05) — absolute-change form because ZN crosses zero and pct-returns explode. Barriers in raw price-sigma units (PT:SL = 2:1, justified by 4.5× MFE/MAE asymmetry). Sigma-floor (no look-ahead): clip(lower = σ.expanding(20).median()×0.2).

Labels sampled at symmetric-CUSUM events (LdP 2018 ch.2 §2.5 Eq.(2.5), κ=1.0σ), pooled across a 6-speed pseudo-cross-section for thickness; SIDE stays single-EMA2. Three gap-handling rules (5.2.a CUSUM reset / 5.2.b σ re-warmup / 5.2.c cross-gap drop) applied to every >5-bd gap.

2. Triggering condition

The meta-classifier (sklearn, 100 trees) emits per-event probability m_t. The position scaler fires continuously: g(m_t) ramps the Martin side size between g_min=0.30 and 1.0. Training labels are triple-barrier outcomes (PT-touch = 1, SL/vertical = 0) over CUSUM events; features led by Kaufman Efficiency Ratio (ER 20d/60d — dominant US10 conditioner, bucket-Sharpe spread 5.64).

\[g(m_t) \in [0.30,\,1.0], \qquad \text{pos}_t = g(m_t)\cdot \mathrm{sign}\big(\text{Martin}_t\big) \]

Cells in sweep: Internal grid (honest DSR N): τ ∈ {0.40, 0.50, 0.55, 0.60} × feature-subset {ER-only, ER+rest} = 8 configs. This run reports feat=ER_plus_rest, τ=0.50.
