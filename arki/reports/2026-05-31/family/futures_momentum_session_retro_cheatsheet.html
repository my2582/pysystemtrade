F5 — Defect → Lesson chain 4 entries · framework evolution log

Each defect produced a durable rule. Reading them together shows how the framework adapted to specific failure modes. Two were caught by owner/reviewer intuition; one by framework itself.

2026-05-29 — sMOM unbounded w → Rule 2 (degenerate-denominator bound)

sMOM reported Sharpe lift +0.255 with all gates PASS. max|w| = 2,927 on 39 days, 6,439× leverage on $50K. Caught by owner question "몇 계약?", not by framework.
Rule: weight formulas with a denominator that can approach zero (1/σ, 1/var, 1/semi_var, ...) MUST declare a hard cap at pre-reg time. Lint validates via regex on degenerate-denominator hints.

2026-05-29 — Martin §2.3 misreading → Rule 1 (Eq + § citation)

Gates cited "Martin Fig 1" as slogan. Paper §1 thesis was opposite of our reading ("skew is property of STRATEGY, not asset"). Caught by external reviewer rereading §2.3 carefully.
Rule: every gate cites paper as <paper> §X.Y Eq.M or §X.Y claim:"...", never Fig N alone. Companion rule: paper-derived gates MUST list assumption_set + empirical assumption_check.

2026-05-31 — G4 control-gate ambiguity → vol-paired diagnostic

sMOM capped: SP500 Sharpe rescued from −0.009 → +0.305 via vol collapse 24.34 → 6.23. G4 ("Sharpe < 0.20") fired FAIL — but this was MECHANISM not bug. Caught by framework's own pre-reg logic execution.
Rule: control gates of form "Sharpe < N on instrument Y" pair with vol diagnostic. If vol_capped/vol_baseline ≤ 0.7 → rescue via vol reduction = MECHANISM_NOTE not FAIL.

2026-05-31 — Rule 2 scope refinement

Original Rule 2 ("all multiplicative weights must declare bound") was too broad — fired false positives on safe forecast-style weights (F/σ̂ where σ̂ has positive floor). Scope-corrected to degenerate-denominator class only.
Rule (refined): bound required when denominator can approach zero on a path the strategy traverses. Constant weights, pre-tuned scalars, F/σ̂ (σ̂ floored) exempt. Cost of declaring unneeded bound = 0; cost of missing needed bound = sMOM 6,439× artifact.
