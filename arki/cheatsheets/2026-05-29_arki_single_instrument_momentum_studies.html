F1 — ProblemSingle-instrument trend on rates futures: what is the real edge?defined four ways
Visual — skew of MARKET returns vs holding period (Martin Fig 1 reproduction)
Vol-normalised market returns (U = ΔX / σ̂_25d), non-overlapping M-day blocks. US10 (rates, solid) peaks positive at M=40-60; SP500 (equity, dashed) stays negative — Martin Fig 1 sign contrast.

Prose

Single-instrument momentum is the structurally weak limit of trend following. Carver's pysystemtrade is built for ~30-100 instruments precisely because per-instrument Sharpe is ~0.2-0.3 and the portfolio-level ~1.0 comes from low cross-correlation. So why study it? Small-AUM operators cannot diversify; they need an honest read on what's actually deliverable on one contract.

Literature

  • Martin (2023), "Design and analysis of momentum trading strategies" (Imperial). §2.3 closed-form: pure trend (all a_j>0) → positive skew of trading returns, peaking at M ∝ indicator response time, decaying as M⁻¹ᐟ². Fig 1: US Treasuries (7-10y) skew positive at intermediate M, SPX negative.
  • Hanauer & Windmüller (2022), "Enhanced Momentum Strategies" (TUM). Three vol-scalers on cross-sectional equity (cMOM Barroso-Santa-Clara 2015; sMOM Wang-Yan 2021; dMOM Daniel-Moskowitz 2016). Conclusion: all reduce crashes; none consistently beats plain MOM in factor tests.

Empirical (this study)

US10 market skew at M=60: +0.20 (peak +0.24 at M=40). SP500 market skew at M=60: -0.04, daily -0.25. Sign contrast reproduced. Conclusion: rates futures is a legitimate single-instrument trend candidate; equity index is not.
F2 — DesignTwo studied variants: baseline EWMAC, baseline + dMOM overlaynative pysystemtrade engine; overlay at returns stream
Baseline — 6-speed EWMAC (Martin "linear strategies §2", equal-weight)
Six EWMAC (=EMA2, Martin §2.1 turnover-optimal kernel) speed pairs at equal forecast weights. Carry rule explicitly off so all a_j ≥ 0 (Martin §2.3 pure-trend hypothesis). Soft forecast cap ±20 — Martin §4 warns that binary sign rules destroy positive skew.

Decoded (post-review corrections in italics)

  • EWMAC = (fast EMA − slow EMA) / σ̂ — inside Martin's linear strategy class (φ_n = Σ a_j U_{n-j}), but with a_j weights derived from an EMA difference of price LEVELS, not price CHANGES as in the paper's EMA2 (Eq. 7-8). The variational optimum te^(−α̇t) ("EMA2=") is a single Laguerre-style kernel; the 6-pair equal-weight stack used here is a coarse approximation. Empirical (Step 3 cap=∞ ablation): peak fixed-M skew ≈ 1.90 vs paper EMA2 prediction ≈ 2.1 — within ~10% but not identical.
  • Equal-weight across 6 speeds replaces cross-instrument diversification with within-instrument pseudo-diversification.
  • Position = forecast / σ̂ × (capital × vol_target / instrument_value_vol), rounded to integer contracts.

Why this design (post-review re-framing)

Originally framed as "Martin §2.3 pure-trend application." Post-review correction: the forecast_cap = 20 in chapter-15 places this baseline in §4 nonlinear class (any cap reduces max skew per §4). Ablation (Step 3, cap=20 vs cap=∞) showed the §4 effect is small on this config but the framing was inverted. Six speeds chosen ex-ante from Carver chapter-15 (no tuning on result) is the only Martin-aligned claim that fully holds.
Overlay — dMOM (Daniel-Moskowitz 2016 / Hanauer 2022)
Dynamic scaler. μ̂_t from expanding OLS R_MOM,t = γ0 + γ_int · I_Bear,t-1 · σ²_RMRF,t-1 + ε; bear lookback 24m; mkt-vol lookback 126d; warmup 504d. λ closed-form so std(R_dMOM)==std(R_MOM). Weight can flip negative in bear+high-vol regimes.

Decoded

  • Designed to reduce momentum crashes by flipping exposure when conditional expected return (μ̂) goes negative.
  • Applied here as a multiplicative scaler on baseline daily strategy returns (equivalent to position scaling under fixed capital and continuous positions).
  • λ tuned ex-ante (closed-form vol match); no grid search.

Why test it on rates

Pre-registered question: does the Hanauer cross-sectional-equity result transfer to a single rates futures? If yes, expect Sharpe lift ≥ +0.10 AND maxDD improvement ≥ +5 pp AND skew_per_trade ≥ 1.0 (preservation).

Martin baseline — 6-speed EWMAC pure trend promoted_post_hoc

Applies to: US10 (rates) and SP500 (equity), single instrument, $50k capital, integer contracts.

1. Core formula tested

Per-rule normalised trend forecast (one of six speeds), then equal-weight combine + soft cap.

Fk,t = (EMAfast,k(Pt) − EMAslow,k(Pt)) / σ̂t
Fcombined,t = clip(Σk wk · scalark · Fk,t, ±20)

k ∈ {2_8, 4_16, 8_32, 16_64, 32_128, 64_256} (fast_span, slow_span pairs); wk = 1/6 each (no tuning); scalark = chapter-15 default (10.6, 7.5, 5.3, 3.75, 2.65, 1.87); σ̂t = pysystemtrade default daily price-vol; ±20 cap = §4 nonlinearity (Paper Eq. predicts capping reduces max skew, contradicts our earlier "Martin-compliant" framing).

paper mapping: linear class instance with aj determined by EMA difference of price LEVELS (not price changes as in paper EMA2). Inside the linear class but ≠ paper's variational optimum te^(−α̇t).

2. Triggering condition

Continuous. Position re-evaluated every business day at close. No event trigger; no entry/exit threshold beyond the combined forecast crossing zero.

post = round(Fcombined,t / 10 · capital · vol_target / instr_value_volt)

vol_target = 20% annualised; buffered position sticky in a band → trade only when desired exits band (turnover control).

3. Action
Trade shapeDiscrete contract adjustment to round(desired). Long/short BOTH used (engine native; see DECISIONS post-review for verdict on per-instrument skew interpretation).
Observation date (T)Close of business day T. EWMAC, vol, forecast all computed on data ≤ T close. No look-ahead.
Trade date (T+1)Open of T+1 (pysystemtrade convention: pandlt+1 uses post · ΔPt→t+1). Lagged-position attribution.
Between trade daysPosition held; daily P&L from post × price change.
Leverage / sizing cap±5 contracts observed max over 1985-2026 at $50k cap, 20% vol_target. ≈ $550k notional / 11× leverage on capital at peak; avg ~3×. Engine has no hard contract cap (vol-target limits naturally).
Re-entry ruleNo re-entry rule — continuous. Sign flips through 0 = exit + new opposite-sign entry.

dMOM overlay — Daniel-Moskowitz dynamic scaler falsified

Applies to: Martin baseline daily strategy returns on US10 and SP500.

1. Core formula tested
wdMOM,t = (1 / 2λ) · μ̂t / σ̂²strategy,t
μ̂t from expanding OLS: RMOM,t = γ0 + γint · IBear,t−1 · σ²RMRF,t−1 + εt

IBear,t−1 = 1 if cumulative 24-mo back-adjusted price change < 0; σ²RMRF = 126d realized variance of vol-normalised market returns; σ̂²strategy = 126d realized variance of baseline daily strategy returns; λ = closed-form so std(R · w) = std(R) full-sample (scalar).

Paper class: §4 nonlinear (ψ(Vn) with Vn = past R²negative, not just past Un). §2.3 Eq. 12 does NOT apply.

2. Triggering condition

Continuous, daily. wdMOM,t computed every day from expanding-window regression up to t−1.

3. Action
Trade shapeMultiplicative scaler on baseline daily returns (NOT on positions). Equivalent to position scaling under fixed capital + continuous positions.
Observation date (T)End of day T. Regression on [start, T−1].
Trade date (T+1)Effective leverage applied to T+1 strategy return.
Between rebalsn/a — daily.
Leverage / sizing capNO declared cap (pre-reg gap; surfaced by sMOM lesson). w can flip negative (bear+high-vol regimes) and is unbounded.
Re-entry ruleSign flip of w = effective position direction flip (overlay).

sMOM overlay — Wang-Yan semi-vol scaler registered_pending_remediation

Applies to: Martin baseline daily strategy returns on US10 and SP500. Status downgraded from `promoted` after unbounded-w finding and Martin §4 confound critique.

1. Core formula tested
wsMOM,t = √(target_var) / √(semi_var126d,t)   /   λ
semi_var126d,t = (1/126) · Σj=1..126 R²baseline,t−j · I{Rbaseline,t−j < 0}

target_var = full-sample mean of R²baseline (scalar; OOS-violation at the level, but invariant for Sharpe/skew/maxDD ratios); λ = closed-form vol-match scalar (similar invariance); sign(w) = +1 always (positive ratio of square roots).

Paper class: §4 nonlinear (ψ(Vn) with Vn = past R²negative). §2.3 Eq. 12 does NOT apply.

Continuous, daily. wsMOM,t computed every day from rolling 126d window strictly before t.

New experiment findings — quick reads

sMOM PROMOTED

Semi-vol scaler lifts US10 Sharpe by +0.255 and improves maxDD by 11 pp without destroying skew (4.69). Mechanism: scale up in low-downside-vol periods (which are common during secular rate-down rallies on US10). Caveat: SP500 control passes at 0.161 (vs 0.20 gate) — borderline; SP500 daily skew degenerate (+106). US10-rates-specific finding; needs replication on Bund / BOBL / OAT before generalising.

Fast-tilt FALSIFIED

Weighting toward fast EWMAC speeds (0.30 ewmac2_8 + 0.25 ewmac4_16 + ...) HURTS US10 Sharpe by -0.155 with 66% more turnover (485 → 806 trades). The "small-AUM speed advantage" (Carver) is theoretical: it requires a cost differential between large/small CTAs, not just a same-cost faster-weight reshuffle. In transaction-cost-free integer-contract sim, faster = more whipsaw. Skew preserved (4.36) so the strategy is still long-option-shaped; just worse mean.

Carry-toggle REFUTED §2.3

Adding carry at weight 0.30 LIFTS US10 Sharpe (+0.090) AND INCREASES skew (5.35 → 7.34, +2.0). The Martin §2.3 implication "carry as non-trend should flatten skew" is refuted on US10: bond futures carry (positive roll yield under backwardation in secular rate-down) is itself a positively-skewed return source, so trend+carry stacks two long-option payoffs rather than diluting one. Single-instrument finding; do not generalise to commodities or FX. Implication: trend+carry is a Path A promotion candidate for US10 with a separate Sharpe-lift gated experiment.

F4 — TradeoffSharpe is structurally low; skew compensates; dMOM does not bend the curvehonest costs
Single-instrument Sharpe vs portfolio Sharpe — the real cost
Carver/Martin per-instrument trend Sharpe ~0.2-0.3; portfolio ~1.0 is bought with breadth, not signal. Single instrument sacrifices 60%+ of Sharpe; dMOM does not recover any.

Tradeoffs (honest)

  • Sharpe ceiling on US10 = ~0.4 gross. Refutes the prior uncited 0.7-1.0 claim. The reward sits in positive trade skew (+4.4) and convex payoffs, not Sharpe.
  • Low win rate (23%). 3 of 4 trades lose money; few large winners drive the equity curve. The strategy requires the operator to ride drawdowns.
  • maxDD geometric -55% on US10 (-85% on SP500) over 43 years. Tail risk is real even with vol targeting.
  • dMOM costs trades: 342 → 537 (+57%) without lifting Sharpe; turnover (and implicit cost) rises with no return improvement.
  • SP500 not a candidate. Equity index single-instrument trend is approximately zero-Sharpe with negative daily skew. Sign-contrast verifies Martin Fig 1 but not as a deployable strategy.

Why dMOM didn't work here

Hanauer's own paper warns: dMOM is a crash-mitigator, not a return-booster. On rates, the worst drawdowns come from sharp rate reversals; the 24-month back-adjusted-price bear indicator is dominated by the slow secular rate-down trend, not by the equity-style regime states the regression was tuned to catch. The mechanism does not transfer.

Pre-registration discipline — one decision per experiment
Lifecycle: research → pre-register (git locked) → run → register → decide → promote (PASS) or falsify (FAIL). Negative results are preserved as first-class artifacts.

Statistical discipline applied here

  • Pre-registered gates (Martin: Path Z post-hoc grandfather, one-time; dMOM: Path A genuine pre-registration locked at commit 65c423bc).
  • No tuned parameters on result. EWMAC speeds inherited from Carver chapter-15; bear/mkt-vol windows fixed ex-ante; λ closed-form vol-match; no grid search per Lopez de Prado AFML Ch.3.
  • Reconciliation gates (sum of trade attribution vs total P&L) PASS on all 4 cells (|diff| < 1.2 pp on a 349 pp cumulative).
  • Sample: 43 years (10,375 daily obs on US10, 10,364 on SP500). Per-trade n = 342 (US10) / 335 (SP500). Not bootstrap-CI'd in this pass; downstream SRP would add CI95/CI99.

Overfitting risk — quantified

Effective free parameters on the result: 0. All hyperparameters fixed ex-ante (6 EWMAC pairs from Carver; vol target 20% default; bear lookback 24m / mkt-vol 126d / warmup 504d from the source papers; λ closed-form). The Martin run is a faithful reproduction; the dMOM run is a transfer test of a published formula. Overfitting concern is structural (single-instrument is small sample of "edges") but not parameter-tuning.
